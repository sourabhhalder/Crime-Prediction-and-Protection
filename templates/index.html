FAQs

1. How accurate is AI Rakshak’s crime risk prediction?

AI Rakshak’s accuracy depends on the quality and recency of historical crime data. Our models are trained on verified, real-world incident reports and achieve 70–90% predictive precision for high-risk areas and time windows. However, no prediction is 100% certain — we always recommend combining AI insights with local safety practices.

2. What data does the machine learning model use?

We use anonymized historical crime records (type, location, time, date, weather, and local events) from police and public safety sources. No personally identifiable information (PII) is stored. The model continuously retrains on new data to adapt to changing crime patterns.

3. Is AI Rakshak designed specifically for women’s and children’s safety?

Yes. Our model includes dedicated risk factors relevant to women and children — such as poorly lit routes, deserted areas at specific hours, and reported harassment hotspots. Users can filter predictions for “safer paths for women” or “child-safe zones” near schools and parks.

4. Does the tool work in low-data or rural areas?

Yes, but with lower granularity. In areas with sparse historical crime data, AI Rakshak uses transfer learning from similar demographic regions and adds community-reported safety flags. We also allow local administrators to seed initial data.

5. Can AI Rakshak predict real-time crimes (e.g., in progress)?

No. The model predicts near-term risk (next 2–6 hours) based on historical patterns and current conditions (time, day, weather, public events). It does not use live surveillance or real-time tracking. For emergencies, always call local law enforcement.

6. How do you prevent bias against certain neighborhoods or communities?

We audit models quarterly for demographic and geographic bias. If the historical data over-polices a specific area, we apply reweighting techniques to avoid unfairly labeling it as high-risk. Users can also flag biased predictions for human review.

7. Can I use AI Rakshak to plan a safe commute for my child?

Absolutely. Parents/guardians can input a start and end point, along with the child’s age and mode of travel (walk, bus, etc.). The model returns time-stamped risk scores for each route segment, highlighting safer alternatives based on historical crime data for minors.

8. How often is the ML model updated with new crime data?

The model receives daily incremental updates from integrated police and public safety APIs. A full retraining occurs every two weeks. Historical data older than 5 years is archived to prevent outdated patterns from influencing current predictions.

9. Is my personal location data stored or shared?

No. AI Rakshak processes location data anonymously in memory during a session. You are never asked for your name, address, or real-time location. Only aggregated, non-identifiable query data may be used to improve the model.

10. Can AI Rakshak replace neighborhood watch or police presence?

No. AI Rakshak is a supplementary risk awareness tool — not a security system or emergency response service. Always stay aware of your surroundings, use community safety networks, and contact local authorities if you feel threatened.
